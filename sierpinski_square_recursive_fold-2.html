3D subdivision rule

mₓᵧ = (aₓᵧ + bₓᵧ) / 2
mᶻ = (aᶻ + bᶻ) / 2 + λ · ℓ · χ(θ)

[a,b,c] → [a,c,m] · [c,b,m]
Each split inserts one lifted midpoint on the tagged edge. Both children use that same 3D point and share the complete edge [c,m]. The two child facets are therefore joined, but are no longer coplanar with the parent. Z depends on the local recursive edge geometry, never on traversal time. The result is a 3D folded-surface embedding of the square-filling path, not a claim to fill a 3D volume.
