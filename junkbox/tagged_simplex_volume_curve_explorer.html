Diameter contraction audit

For the tetrahedral barycentric matrices, all binary products of each length were enumerated exactly using integer arithmetic. δ is the Dobrushin coefficient; it bounds Euclidean simplex diameter through the convex-combination coupling inequality.

Product lengthMaximum δMeaning

Every 8 consecutive bisections contract every descendant diameter by at least 1/2.

This is not a shape-regularity guarantee. Tetrahedra can become sliver-like; shrinking diameter, not bounded aspect ratio, is the property used for convergence.
